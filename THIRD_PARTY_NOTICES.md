## Carried by the Docker image and by installs

These are installed from npm or crates.io, not copied into this repository. They are named here because their licenses ask for attribution, or (libvips) say what a recipient of the binary is owed.

### sharp and libvips (LGPL-3.0-or-later)

`@nbo/serve` (next/image optimization) and Next.js use [sharp](https://github.com/lovell/sharp) (Apache-2.0, Copyright Lovell Fuller and contributors). On install, sharp brings a prebuilt binary of [libvips](https://github.com/libvips/libvips), the `@img/sharp-libvips-<platform>` package (for example `@img/sharp-libvips-linux-x64` in the Docker image, version 1.3.4 with sharp 0.35.5). libvips is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later (LGPL-3.0-or-later), Copyright John Cupitt and others.

- The source of libvips is at https://github.com/libvips/libvips (the license is its `LICENSE` file; the full LGPL-3.0 text is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt, and the GPL-3.0 it builds on at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt).
- The exact sources, versions and build scripts of the prebuilt binary, and of the other libraries it bundles with their own licenses, are at https://github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips (tag `v1.3.4`, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`).
- NBO does not modify libvips and links it dynamically, through sharp, as a separate shared library. You may replace it: install sharp against a libvips you build yourself (see sharp's installation guide, "Custom libvips"), or rebuild the Docker image with a different `@img/sharp-libvips-*` package.
